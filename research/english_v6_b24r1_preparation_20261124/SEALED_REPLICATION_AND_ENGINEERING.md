## Measured archive capacity

ZIPs of 154,431,316 bytes and their exact original metadata/logs. Existing
retained TRAIN/LIVE occupies 50,759,903 bytes. The fixed synthetic source
bound is 109,588,224 bytes. A conservative example using 240 MB of complete
PRIMARY proof, 32 MB metadata and 10 MB whole natural audio yields a ZIP
envelope of 524,411,765 bytes, exceeding the former replica source limit.

Only the new replica SOURCE limit is 768,000,000 bytes. The replica quality
producer remains limited to 512,000,000 bytes, the nested primary SOURCE to
512,000,000 bytes, and other original artifacts to 128,000,000 bytes. The
pre-consumption capacity check computes the actual authenticated proof size
and the twenty-four original FLAC frame counts before allowing generation;
an oversized bound still stops. Compression and expanded size, hashes, CRC,
unique members and safe paths remain required. No acoustic gate is changed.
