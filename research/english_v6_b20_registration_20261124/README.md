# B20 complete preregistration: a new source distribution

This source-only experiment retains the four exact B17 PT files, the original
B14 quality function and gains, and the original five native observer models.
The source format changes to the predeclared positive length-scale pair
**1.4 / 1.8**; the ordinary question/request scales remain **1.0 / 1.08**.
It is a new complete experiment. B19's baseline and prospective arms both
produced 16/16 valid native outputs, so no causal repair of the B17 or B18
failures has been established.

The original B19 three-archive feasibility proof is pinned by run, head, byte
length, ZIP SHA256, original producer code and the exact saved result. Every
B20 source readback retains and revalidates those three original ZIPs. It
recomputes all 48 original Parakeet returns and the fixed feasibility rule;
matching self-reported success fields are insufficient. B19 originals remain
diagnostic-only and permanently excluded from KWS and training.

## Complete source ancestry

The old 44 original archive locks remain unchanged. B20 adds the authenticated
full `train_context24` source ZIP and the three original B19 source/native/
readback ZIPs, giving **48 original archives** to authenticate before TTS.
The full context24 supplement contributes 24 PCM and 12 source groups; its 12
canonical readers were already excluded by older protocols. All 48 B19 PCM
and eight planned canary identities are also excluded. The exact combined
frozen sets contain **2,247 PCM, 356 source groups and 276 canonical people**.
Their complete sorted-set SHA256 digests are in `assessment.json`.

The new B19 and context24 original ZIPs were independently read locally. The
earlier original inventory is bound to the authentic saved source history;
this registration does not claim a new local read of every old ZIP. The
one-shot Actions preflight downloads and verifies all 48 bodies again before
any new Piper request. Old frozen history files and STOP outcomes are not
rewritten.

## Fixed future sources and gates

Primary synthetic voices are **798–821**, with four whole originals each.
Replication synthetic voices **822–845** are separately reserved. Exact
canonical mappings, native text/IPA and seeds are recorded before source or
KWS execution. No padding, cropping, rerender, retry, third rate or original
replacement is permitted.

Primary requires **five valid complete native observations for every one of
the 96 originals**. Empty, missing or invalid payloads stop the whole panel.
The B19 diagnostic empty-return exception is absent. After full raw evidence
verification, the unchanged source admission function must still yield at
least **12 positives, 24 ordinary originals and 8 paired source groups**.
The fixed existing B17 natural primary is reused without observation: its
24 slots retain 21 admitted originals from 11 readers and three UNKNOWN rows.

The old nine `dev-other` replica readers remain sealed. The new reserve is
drawn from the already retained official `SPEAKERS.TXT` metadata for
`train-clean-100`, with full historical, all-Piper, sealed and new-cohort
canonical exclusions. Of 251 official readers, 239 remain eligible. A fixed
salt ranks the nine reserved readers:

`3982, 1116, 4137, 3374, 5192, 40, 1898, 6019, 2391`.

Each reader has two whole-utterance slots under a fixed transcript eligibility
and SHA256 rank rule; shortage or later quality rejection cannot select a
replacement reader or utterance. The original **12 admitted ordinary
utterances / 8 admitted readers** minima and five-observer policy remain.
No new natural audio has been downloaded or decoded. Official archive byte
length was checked with a HEAD request only; SHA256 and MD5 come from the
linked official indexes retained in `natural-reserve.json`.

## Execution boundary

The source workflow retains an inert exact-parent placeholder. A separately
reviewed, first public successor event is required to open its one source
run. It authenticates the original B19 producers, all source history and
canonical voice maps before the first of at most 96 public Piper synthesis
requests, then allows at most 96 calls per native observer. Internal TTS
neural/ONNX forwards are not instrumented and remain null.

This source workflow contains no KWS scoring, optimization or promotion
step. Those evaluation input locks remain disabled/null until the actual
new 96-original source and all five original native observer archives pass
verification. Opening the newly reserved replication additionally requires
an authenticated B20 primary numerical PASS under the unchanged B14 rule.
All old replicas, product CAL/LOCKED and `test-other` remain untouched.
