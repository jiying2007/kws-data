## Termination and stop semantics

The actual upstream `talker.generate` return is observed once. The wrapper requires one nonempty sequence, matching hidden-state/sequence iterations, a single final EOS token 2150, no earlier EOS, and strictly fewer than 120 iterations. It rejects invalid termination before upstream decoding. Successful return/load-only is not an EOS substitute. The official 1,920-sample codec upsampling relation binds `(iterations - 1)` to native frames; the independent 12-second waveform bound remains in force.

Every failure, duration/token/output limit, malformed waveform or unproven EOS stops the fixed loop. No failed cell is reissued. If a child dies after writing a claim but before updating its ledger, the post-exit finalizer preserves that claim and marks the cell consumed/failed. It never infers completion from audio/EOS, never executes a model, and retains all six denominator rows. Invalid incomplete WAV bytes and unfinished atomic metadata saves are retained privately, with only fixed-name hash/length/reason receipts allowed into the public package. Complete rows cannot be repaired or silently downgraded. Corrupt/non-prefix claims fail closed. Killing the host itself or a runner disappearance can still prevent final publication; that does not authorize a rerun.
