What can be triaged from text
The categories describe ordinary dictionary readings of ASR text relative to
intended text. They do not describe what the waveform actually contains.
“Segmental agreement” below means equal tone-stripped dictionary pinyin for
the differing characters, never an acoustic phonetic measurement. Lexical
tones are kept separately. A word-level reading does not establish its realized
tone, duration, articulation, word boundary, or prosody in connected speech.
Unknown is retained for missing/incomplete ASR, unreviewed readings, or changes
requiring insertion/deletion alignment. Even lexical_match proves only text
equality. Acoustic content and pronunciation remain UNKNOWN for all six rows.
